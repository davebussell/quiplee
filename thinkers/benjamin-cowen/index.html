Thinkers/Benjamin Cowen

Crypto analyst, Into The Cryptoverse

Benjamin Cowen

Runs the Into The Cryptoverse YouTube channel and research service. He popularised the "bull market support band" for Bitcoin: the 20-week simple and 21-week exponential moving averages read together.

What they argue

  • In a healthy bull market, price holds above the band and corrections tend to find support at it.
  • Losing the band on a weekly close has historically preceded extended weakness.
  • He has also said that in the current cycle the 50-week average has been the more reliable support.

Authors of Time Series Momentum (2012)

Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen

Tobias Moskowitz, Yao Hua Ooi and Lasse Heje Pedersen documented time-series momentum in the Journal of Financial Economics in 2012: an asset's own past 12-month excess return predicts its next month, across equity, bond, currency and commodity futures.

What they argue

  • Trends persist: assets that rose over the last year tend to keep rising in the near term, then partly reverse over longer horizons.
  • A diversified time-series momentum portfolio performed best in extreme markets.

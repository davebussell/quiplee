Stan Weinstein

Technical analyst whose 1988 book laid out Stage Analysis: every stock cycles through basing, advancing, topping and declining stages, and the 30-week moving average tells you which one you are in.

What they argue

  • Buy in Stage 2, when price breaks above a 30-week average that has flattened and turned up.
  • Get out when price closes back below the 30-week average; never hold a Stage 4 decline.

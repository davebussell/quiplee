month-end rule

Time-series momentum (12-month)

From Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen·Authors of Time Series Momentum (2012)

Hold the asset while its return over the last 12 months beats Treasury bills. Otherwise sit in bills.

How the rule works

  1. In when the 12-month return at month-end beats the 12-month T-bill return.
  2. Out, in T-bills, when it doesn't.

The paper also goes short. Quiplee tests the long-only version an individual investor can run.

The case against

Valeriy Zakamulin tested moving-average timing on up to 155 years of U.S. stock data and found no statistically significant edge in the second half of that record. The rules earned their keep in a few severe bear markets and lagged buy and hold for long stretches in between.

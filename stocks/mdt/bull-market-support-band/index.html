An in call is right when the price rose before the next call; an out call is right when it fell. The latest 14 of 110 calls shown.

How the rule works

  1. In when a weekly close is above both averages.
  2. Out when a weekly close is below both averages.
  3. Inside the band, the rule keeps its last call.

Weeks end on Friday for stocks and ETFs, and on Sunday for crypto.

The case against

Valeriy Zakamulin tested moving-average timing on up to 155 years of U.S. stock data and found no statistically significant edge in the second half of that record. The rules earned their keep in a few severe bear markets and lagged buy and hold for long stretches in between.
